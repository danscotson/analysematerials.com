About analyseMaterials

Transparent, practical software for materials characterisation.

analyseMaterials is a software portfolio of tools that support materials characterisation, microscopy analysis, diffraction visualisation and reproducible research.

Many materials analysis workflows rely on a mixture of software and ignore the importance of communicating these results. analyseMaterials software combines analysis and visualisation to extract more value from your datasets.

Current focus

  • Microscopy image analysis
  • X-ray diffraction visualisation
  • Scientific figure preparation
  • Materials data workflows
